## Features

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Steam Workshop" icon="rocket">
    Browse, search and subscribe to Workshop mods inside the app, without switching windows.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mod suites" icon="setting">
    Save mods as separate "mod suites" for each play style and switch with one click. You can also import an existing Better Mod Loader (BML) setup.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Launch the game" icon="right-caret">
    Start Escape from Duckov straight from SuperDucky, without switching to another app.
  </Card>
  <Card title="One place for everything" icon="puzzle">
    Local mods, Workshop subscriptions and NuGet mods share one list, with groups, drag-and-drop ordering, history restore and health checks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="NuGet market" icon="download">
    Once enabled, browse and install mods published on NuGet. It is also where SuperDucky.Connector, which the save gallery needs, comes from.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Save gallery" icon="open-book">
    Read your game save, show collected items by category, and compare the attributes of up to 6 items.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
